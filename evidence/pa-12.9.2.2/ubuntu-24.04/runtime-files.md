# Runtime files

- `/opt/jitterbit/bin/jitterbit`: local service control and status.
- `/opt/jitterbit/bin/jitterbit-config`: present, but unused for the qualified no-proxy path.
- `/opt/jitterbit/Resources/register.json`: native registration input.
- `/opt/jitterbit/Resources/credentials.txt`: persistent product-managed identity evidence.
- Bundled JRE keytool and cacerts: observed; optional trust mutation remains unqualified.
